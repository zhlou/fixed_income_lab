What this source describes

Historical estimates of an off-the-run nominal Treasury curve. Zero and instantaneous forward rates are continuously compounded; par rates use nominal semiannual coupons. Today’s file is a revised historical vintage. Exact historical availability times are unknown. Model-derived prices use the selected snapshot.

The Lab restricts historical discounting to the published maturity range, beginning at one year. A coupon payment sooner than that prevents a complete historical price. The payment table identifies each affected amount.
