Numerical check · does a smaller bump agree?

Bumps are ±1, ±0.5, and ±0.25 bp. Regular-period results have an analytical reference. Dated results use ±0.5 bp and the original dirty-price pricer; smaller bumps eventually suffer rounding cancellation. Macaulay duration is not reported for this dated convention.
